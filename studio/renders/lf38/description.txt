After three weeks of failed sketches, an idea arrives in the shower. Where did it come from? In this calm, sourced sleep-friendly deep dive we follow one imagined illustrator, Mira, and look at what science says about creative ideas: the eureka myth, the four stages of thought, Poincaré on a bus, why we get stuck, why stepping away helps, the wandering mind, walking, the aha moment in the brain, sleeping on it, and the creative edge of sleep. The second half is a long, gentle wind-down to help you fall asleep.
