- Voice: Kokoro blend `am_onyx+bm_lewis+bm_lewis` (mid D, warm), speed 0.88, 1-2 sentences per take, jitter, pauses by length / "?", 12 ms fades, `flow.warm()` EQ. Code: studio/voice/flow.py, studio/assetedit/longform.py `voice()`.
