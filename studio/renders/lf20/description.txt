How do people really learn, and how can you learn almost anything faster and remember it for longer? A calm, sourced listen through the research: why rereading and highlighting barely work, the power of testing yourself, spacing, mixing topics, the trap of feeling sure, explaining to learn, deliberate practice and the ten thousand hour myth, mindsets, memory palaces, notes and reading, working memory, brain training, and sleep. Every claim comes from named studies, and the doubts are left in.
