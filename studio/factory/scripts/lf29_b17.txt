@char A10 living_room_day | Your voice is normal | Whatever it's like
The first is simple. Whatever your inner voice is like, constant or rare, chatty or quiet, words or pictures, it's a normal way to have a mind. The beeper studies showed how different we all are on the inside. There's no right amount of inner speech.

@char A13 attic_rain | It's a tool | Not a judge

None of these is a magic switch. Like the athletes' self-talk, they seem to work better with practice. So be patient with yourself. If you forget, or if the critic comes back, that's not failing. It's just a cue to try again, gently, the next time.
