Why do old habits follow us, even when we really want to change? In this calm, sourced deep dive we follow one imagined woman, Ines, through her first months in a new flat in a new city, as her hand keeps reaching for a light switch that isn't there. We look at what a habit really is, how the brain hands repeated actions over to autopilot, why people keep going for a reward they no longer want, why good intentions change so little, why moving house opens a rare window for change, why people with strong self-control resist less, phone-checking habits, what helps against a strong habit, why stress and tiredness pull us back to old routines, how long a new habit really takes, if-then plans, small bits of friction, replacing instead of erasing, and being kind to yourself after a slip.
