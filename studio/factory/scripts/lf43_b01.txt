@quote A19 cafe_sunset | Loneliness is the gap between the connection we want and the connection we have. | Peplau and Perlman, early 1980s
Psychologists Letitia Anne Peplau and Daniel Perlman, working in the early 1980s, described loneliness in a way that has stayed useful ever since. It's the painful gap between the connection we want and the connection we have. Notice what that doesn't say. It doesn't mention how many people are around you, or how many names are in your phone.
