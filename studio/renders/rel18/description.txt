Simon Peter was a fisherman from Galilee who denied his teacher three times on the night of the arrest, and then became one of the first leaders of the early church. Belief Through Time follows the Gospels, the Book of Acts and Paul's own letters to ask what they report about the call by the lake, the new name Rock, Caesarea Philippi, the courtyard and the rooster, the empty tomb, Pentecost, Cornelius, the prison escape, the quarrel at Antioch and Peter's last years in Rome, what archaeologists found in Capernaum and beneath Saint Peter's Basilica, and how Catholic, Orthodox, Protestant, Jewish and Muslim traditions remember him.
