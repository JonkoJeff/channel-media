Fed every 8 h by the trend scan (RUNBOOK part T). Patterns, never copies. Outlier = views ÷ channel subscribers (last 7 days).

## Rules
(A pattern becomes a rule after it shows up in 2+ scans. Each rule: first seen / last seen. Remove after 2 weeks unseen.)
- R1 Psych long thumbnail: white or cream/paper background, 2–4 huge words in black with ONE word in red (or on a red block), one simple illustrated figure on the other side showing the emotion. (2026-10-11 00:50 / 2026-10-11 11:22)
- R2 Thumbnail text is the emotional twist, never the title ("Then They Look Away", "Nothing To Prove", "Never Coming Back"). (00:50 / 11:22)
- R3 Psych long title: a quiet, everyday behaviour of a group: "Why Some People [odd habit]" / "Psychology of People Who [don't / skip X]". (00:50 / 11:22)
- R4 Psych Shorts: a 6–15 s static numbered list of 6–7 facts, one highlighted phrase per line, 🧠 in the title. We only use facts we can source; the viral lists are full of fake "studies". (00:50 / 11:22)
- R5 Money/AI long thumbnail: face or character + one huge number or "FREE / ₹0 / $0" + a tool logo or proof screen. For us: only real, public proof; never invented income. (00:50 / 11:22)
