@study A13 living_room_day | Leavitt and Christenfeld, 2011 | University of California, San Diego | Higher average enjoyment with external spoilers
For the externally spoiled stories, average enjoyment ratings were higher than for the unspoiled versions in each of the three story categories. The opening question now has an answer within these experiments: knowing the outcome did not prevent enjoyment, and the externally spoiled versions received higher average ratings.
