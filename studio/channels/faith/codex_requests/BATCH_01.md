Attach to every task and copy exactly: the approved panels `studio/channels/faith/panels/` adam_eve.png, baby_moses.png, jacob_ladder.png, prodigal_son.png, peter_water.png, noah_ark.png, joseph_brothers.png, jericho.png, red_sea.png, david_sling.png, jonah_fish.png, dg_01.png, and the character sheets in `studio/channels/faith/sheets/` (david_angles, goliath_angles, noah_angles, jona_angles).

## Rules for every image
- 9:16 vertical (1024x1536 or 941x1672). NO text, NO numbers, NO captions, NO speech bubbles, NO logos, NO borders, NO watermark.
- Built for animation: clean sky in the top 30% (the camera tilts, clouds drift), main action in the middle, calm ground in the bottom 25% (captions go there), subject inside the middle 80% of the width. Vary wide shots, low angles, close-ups, back and side views.
- Kid-friendly: no blood, no injuries, nothing scary. Never draw God or the Prophet Muhammad (the burning bush is only a bush on fire).
- After painting, check: no text anywhere, same character as the reference, 9:16. Repaint once if needed.
