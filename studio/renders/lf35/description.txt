The new thing feels wonderful, and then, a few weeks later, you barely notice it. In this calm, sourced deep dive we follow one imagined person, Nora, and the sea view she stopped seeing, and go myth versus science, point by point, through why happiness never stays: the hedonic treadmill and what later research corrected, why the brain adapts at all, why we are so bad at predicting what will make us happy, how much of happiness is in your genes, why final choices feel better than open ones, why frequent small joys beat rare big ones, how endings shape memories, why breaks and absence make good things new again, what gratitude and savouring really do, why other people matter so much, and why chasing happiness can backfire. Every claim comes from named studies, with the doubts left in. This video is for curiosity, not therapy or medical advice.
