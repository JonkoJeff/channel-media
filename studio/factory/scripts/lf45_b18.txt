@chapter A13 cabin_fireplace | A misty valley | Warm water, cool air
And now, imagine a valley in the early morning, high up in the mountains, where the air is cool and the ground is warm. Mist rises from natural pools of warm water, and drifts across the valley floor like slow breath. Pine trees stand along the slopes, dark green, and above them the sky is just beginning to turn pink.

@char A19 attic_rain | The path | A wooden walkway
A narrow wooden walkway leads you down into the valley, the planks worn smooth and silvery with age. Each step makes a soft, hollow sound. There's no one else about. The air smells of pine and warm stone and wet earth. With each step you take, you feel a little lighter, as if the valley were quietly taking things from your shoulders.

And here is the valley, spread out beneath you. The pools steam gently, each in a bowl of dark stone. A small wooden bridge crosses a narrow stream. The mist hangs in soft, rolling layers, catching the pink and blue of the sky. Everything is quiet. Everything is slow. It's a place where nobody is in a hurry, and nothing has to be done.

@char A10 bedroom_night | The warm water | Easing in
Imagine easing into one of the pools, slowly, the warm water rising around your ankles, your knees, your waist, and then your shoulders. It's exactly the right temperature. Feel how your muscles let go as the warmth reaches them, one at a time. The weight of your arms floats away. The tension in your neck melts like a small cube of butter.

@list A18 attic_rain | Letting go, place by place | Your feet ;; Your calves ;; Your back ;; Your shoulders ;; Your face
Notice each place as it softens. Your feet, warm and loose. Your calves, heavy and easy. Your back, supported by smooth stone. Your shoulders, sinking low. And your face, calm and smooth, with the cool air on your cheeks and the warm water below. Each part lets go in its own time, and none of it needs your help.

@char A13 cabin_fireplace | Steam rising | Slow curls
Watch the steam rise from the water in slow curls, lifting and thinning and disappearing into the cool air. Each curl is a little thought, if you like, rising and fading. There's no need to catch any of them. The valley is full of rising steam, and none of it is lost, and none of it is kept. It simply rises, the way it always has.

@quote A19 rooftop_dusk | Warmth does not need to be understood to be felt. | A thought in the water
Warmth does not need to be understood to be felt. Nobody ever had to remember how the warm water works in order to be soothed by it. The body simply receives it. And the mind, if it wants to, can receive it in the same way, without a single question.

Somewhere in the pines, a bird calls once, a soft, clear note, and is quiet. Then, after a long pause, it calls again, a little further away. The sound travels across the valley and fades into the mist. It's not a message. It's just a gentle sound in a gentle place, and you can let it come and go.

@char A13 bedroom_night | The sky changes | Pink and gold
As you lie in the warm water, the sky overhead is slowly changing. The pink deepens into gold, and the gold into pale blue, while the stars, one by one, fade behind the light. It's a very slow change, so slow you can only tell it's happening by looking away and looking back. And you're in no hurry to see it finish.

@char A19 cabin_fireplace | Heavy and calm | Deep rest
Your whole body is heavy and warm now, as heavy as the stones that line the pool. Your breathing is long and slow. Your thoughts, if there are any, are as soft and unhurried as the steam. You have nowhere to be and nothing to figure out. The valley is looking after you, and everything is as it should be.

@char A10 attic_rain | A cool breath | The air on your face
Now and then, a cool breath of air drifts across your face from the pines, a fresh, quiet touch against the warmth. It's a lovely contrast, the cool above and the warm below. It reminds you that you're here, in your body, in this peaceful place, with nothing asked of you at all.

@char A19 cabin_fireplace | Smooth stones | At the edge of the pool
At the edge of the pool, there's a row of smooth, round stones, warm from the water. Imagine resting your hand on one of them. It's heavy, and smooth, and perfectly still. It's been here a long time, and it'll be here a long time more, and it has never once needed to remember anything. There's a deep calm in stones, and you're welcome to borrow some of it.

@char A10 attic_rain | The slow light | Dawn over the pines
Over the pines, the first thin light of dawn is spreading, pale gold along the ridge. It doesn't hurry. It rises as slowly as the steam, a little more each minute, until the mountain tops are glowing. But you don't need to wake for it. You can lie in the warm water and let the day begin on its own, somewhere far off, like a story told in another room.

@char A13 bedroom_night | Deep, easy breaths | Warm and slow
Your breath is long and slow, and each one seems to carry you a little deeper into the warmth. In, and the water seems to hold you up. Out, and your whole body lets go a little more. The steam curls around you, and the pines whisper, and the whole valley seems to be breathing along with you, in a single, slow, shared rhythm.

@char A19 cabin_fireplace | Content | Nothing missing
There's a particular kind of contentment that comes in warm water, in cool air, in a quiet valley, when nothing is missing. No word, no name, no answer. You've everything you need. The warmth is enough. The quiet is enough. And you are enough, exactly as you are, lying here, doing nothing, while the world turns gently towards the morning.
