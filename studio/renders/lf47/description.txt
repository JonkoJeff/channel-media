You forgot a whole presentation by evening, but you remember every second of a friend's three-minute story about a runaway dog. And a book you're not even sure you like is keeping you up past one in the morning. In this calm, sourced, sleep-friendly deep dive we follow one imagined woman, Hana, through a night of one more chapter, as she wonders why stories grip the mind so hard. We finish with a few gentle, research-based ideas and then a long, slow guided wind-down to help you fall asleep.
