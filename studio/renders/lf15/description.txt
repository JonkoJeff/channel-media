In the summer of 1954, twenty two boys arrived at a camp in Oklahoma, not knowing that the adults around them were running an experiment. Tonight, we'll open the case file of the Robbers Cave study step by step: how strangers became a team, how two teams became rivals, what brought them back together, and the earlier camp that almost nobody heard about for decades.
