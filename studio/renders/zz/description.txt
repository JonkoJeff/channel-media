Someone says something rude, you freeze, and three hours later in the shower you come up with the perfect reply. There's even a name for it: l'esprit de l'escalier, staircase wit. In this video we look at why stress shuts down the part of your brain that comes up with clever answers, why the comeback shows up later, and what you can do in the moment instead.
