# BAD demand signal: YouTube "Most searched terms" (owner screenshot, 22 Oct 2026)
Use these as topic / title / tag input for new BAD long videos and Shorts. Facts must still be sourced (named studies, no invented numbers).
## Use (fits the channel)
1. interesting psychology experiments -> series "experiments you can't unsee" (named studies, one per Short)
2. your brain is not showing you reality -> perception / constructed reality (colour, blind spot, change blindness, rubber hand)
3. completely wrong facts everyone still believes -> myths debunked (learning styles, 10% brain, 21-day habit, goldfish memory) - only with a real source
4. shattered assumptions theory (Janoff-Bulman) -> trauma and beliefs, careful, no clinical advice
5. prosocial behaviour in psychology -> bystander effect, helping studies, Robbers Cave
6. the power of reverse psychology -> reactance (Brehm), forbidden-fruit studies
7. psychology of intelligence / learn anything -> learning science (spacing, retrieval, growth-mindset caveats)
8. how to have impossible conversations -> conflict / listening studies, ONLY research-based, no coaching claims
9. psychology of social media (not marketing) -> attention, variable rewards, social comparison. No money/finance angle.
## Skip
- "the courage to be disliked podcast", "crazy days and nights podcast": searches for someone else's brand/book, no topic for us.
- "psychology of social media marketing": money angle, owner rule says no money/finance content.
## Rule
Put the searched phrase (or a close natural version) in the video title/description and first Short hook when it is true to the content. Re-check this list monthly.
