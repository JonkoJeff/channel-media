Your heart pounds, your mouth goes dry, and your mind goes blank at exactly the wrong moment. In this calm, sourced deep dive we follow one imagined person, Jonah, through one stressful Monday and use it to explore what stress really does to your brain and body: where the word came from (Cannon and Selye), the alarm that fires in seconds, the slower wave of cortisol, why stress can sharpen or scramble your thinking, how it shapes memory, why a sense of control matters so much, what happens when the alarm never switches off, the surprising science of the stress mindset, why a hand to hold calms the brain, and gentle, research-based things that help. Every claim comes from named studies, with the doubts left in. This video is for curiosity, not medical advice. The second half is a slow guided wind-down, so you can let the day go and drift off to sleep.
