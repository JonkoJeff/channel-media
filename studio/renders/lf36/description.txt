A message from someone you love, a long day, an empty stomach, and suddenly you're furious. In this calm, sourced deep dive we follow one imagined person, Sam, through one evening and one unsent message, and explore why we get angry: what anger does in the body, what it's for, why hunger, short sleep and heat make it easier, why venting backfires, and the small, research-based steps that actually help. Settle in, it ends with a gentle wind-down.
