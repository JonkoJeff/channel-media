DESC: What were the names that Adam was taught? Belief Through Time follows Quran 2:30-34: the announcement of a successor on the earth, the lesson of the names, all of them (2:31), the question put to the angels, their famous reply, we have no knowledge except what You have taught us (2:32), Adam informing them (2:33) and the prostration and refusal of Iblis (2:34). We hear the early answers collected by al-Tabari (the names of all things, of the angels, of Adam's descendants, and the debate over one pronoun), Ibn Kathir and the hadith of intercession in Sahih al-Bukhari, the two readings of what was concealed, al-Razi on knowledge and honour, the old debate over whether prophets or angels rank higher, the thread of teaching from the names to the pen (96:1-5) and clear speech (55:1-4), the knowledge given to David, Solomon, Joseph, Jesus and the Prophet, the theologians' and grammarians' debate over whether language is instituted or agreed (al-Ashari, Abu Hashim, Ibn Jinni), modern readings by Muhammad Asad and Maududi, and Sufi readings of the divine names. Then the neighbouring traditions: Adam naming the animals in Genesis 2:19-20, the unnamed heavens of Enuma Elish, the angels who fail the test of names in Genesis Rabbah 17:4, Plato's Cratylus and Philo of Alexandria. This episode closes our series on the Quran's account of creation.
