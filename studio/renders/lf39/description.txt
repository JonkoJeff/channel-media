One song, one quiet moment, and a shiver runs down your arms. Why? In this calm, sourced deep dive we follow one imagined listener, Theo, through a single evening of music and look at what science says about musical chills: what goosebumps are, who gets them, the brain's reward system and dopamine, the role of expectation and surprise, why sad songs can feel so good, how songs carry memories, and why every human culture sings. The last part is a long, gentle wind-down to help you fall asleep.
