If it were useless, it would be strange for almost everyone to carry it around all day. Before we go on, a quiet promise. The science of inner speech is a young field, and it is full of open questions. It is hard to study something that only one person can hear. Where there is a solid result, I'll tell you who found it and when it was published. Where researchers still disagree, I'll say so. You don't need to take anything on trust.

@list A13 attic_rain | Why it's hard to study | It happens in private ;; Only you can report it ;; It fades when you look at it
That's the heart of the difficulty. If I want to study your eyesight, I can show you a chart.
