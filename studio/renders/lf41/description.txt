Why do we put off the things that matter most? In this calm, sourced deep dive we follow one imagined man, Ben, through the week before his sister's wedding, with a speech he keeps not writing, and test five popular beliefs about procrastination against the research: what procrastination really is, laziness versus mood repair, why we treat our future selves like strangers, perfectionism and impulsiveness, a simple formula for motivation, working under pressure, deadlines, health and bedtime procrastination, self-forgiveness, waiting for the right mood, structured procrastination, and small ideas that seem to help, like tiny first steps, if-then plans and temptation bundling.
