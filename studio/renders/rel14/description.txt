On the night before his arrest, Jesus shared a final meal with his disciples in Jerusalem. Religion Unfolded follows the earliest sources, from Paul's letter to the four Gospels, to ask what the Last Supper was, how the accounts differ, and how a single meal became a ritual practised across the world. Along the way, we distinguish what the texts report, what historians can say, and what later traditions built.
