TITLE: The Tower of Babel: When Humanity Was Divided
DESC: On a plain in Shinar, Genesis describes a community building a city and a tower, hoping to remain together and make a name for itself. We explore the story’s carefully chosen words, the monuments of ancient Babylon, and the different questions Jewish and Christian readers have brought to this brief passage. Along the way, we distinguish the biblical account, later interpretation, archaeological evidence, and what remains uncertain.
