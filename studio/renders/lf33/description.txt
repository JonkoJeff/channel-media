Stay, or go? One sheet of paper, two columns, and a decision that won't make itself. In this calm, sourced deep dive we follow one imagined person, Mara, through one long evening at her kitchen table, and use her choice to explore how your brain really makes decisions: fast and slow thinking, why feelings are part of reason (Damasio's patient Elliot), the shortcuts your mind takes, anchoring and framing, the pull of defaults, too much choice and the hungry judge debate, when to trust your gut, the brain signals before a choice (Libet), choice blindness and the reasons we invent afterwards, other people's influence, why we misjudge future feelings, how risk feels, regret, and a few gentle tools for choosing well, including what a coin flip can tell you. Every claim comes from named studies, with the doubts left in. At the end there is a slow, gentle wind-down, so you can listen until you drift off.
