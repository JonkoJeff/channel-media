For the herdr Claude: run this with Codex (`herdr agent start <name> --kind codex`, one task per story below), then commit the PNGs to branch `cosmozibs` under `studio/channels/faith/panels/` and push. Claude (cloud) animates them into 60+ second TikToks.

## THE MODEL (most important, never deviate)
Style and characters must match these approved images exactly (attach them to every task):
`studio/channels/faith/panels/` adam_eve.png, baby_moses.png, jacob_ladder.png, prodigal_son.png, peter_water.png, noah_ark.png, joseph_brothers.png, jericho.png, red_sea.png, david_sling.png, jonah_fish.png, dg_01.png
plus the character sheets in `studio/channels/faith/sheets/` (david_angles, goliath_angles, noah_angles, jona_angles).
- Flat 2D cartoon, thick dark outlines, flat colours with one soft shadow tone, simple rounded-rectangle heads, big round white eyes with small black pupils, compact bodies, simple hands.
- Clear blue sky with a few soft white clouds, warm sand/earth tones, calm backgrounds with little detail.
- The same character must look identical in every shot (face, hair, beard, clothes, colours).

## Rules for every image
- ONE vertical image per shot, 9:16 (1024x1536 or 941x1672). NO text, NO numbers, NO captions, NO speech bubbles, NO logos, NO borders.
- Composition for animation: big clean sky in the top 30% (the camera tilts and the clouds drift), main action in the middle, calm ground in the bottom 25% (captions go there), subject inside the middle 80% of the width.
- Vary the camera: wide shots, low angles for power, close-ups for emotion, back views, side views.
- Kid-friendly: no blood, no injuries, nothing scary. Never draw God or the Prophet Muhammad.
- Save as `studio/channels/faith/panels/<story>_NN.png` (NN = 01, 02, ...).

## Stories (12 shots each = one 60-75 s TikTok)
1. `noah` — Noah's Ark: Noah hears the warning and looks up; building the ark with a hammer; neighbours laughing; animals walking in two by two (elephants, giraffes, lions, sheep); dark clouds and the first rain; the ark on a stormy sea; inside the ark with animals; the rain stops; Noah sends out a dove; the dove returns with an olive leaf; the ark on a mountain top; a rainbow over everyone.
2. `jonah` — Jonah: Jonah hears he must go to Nineveh; he runs the other way to a harbour; on a ship; a big storm with huge waves; sailors afraid; Jonah tells them to throw him in; he splashes into the sea; the big fish opens its mouth; inside the dark fish, Jonah praying by a tiny light; the fish spits him onto a beach; Jonah walking to the big city of Nineveh; the people of Nineveh listening.
3. `moses` — Moses: baby in a basket in the reeds; the princess finds him; grown-up Moses as a shepherd; the burning bush (a bush on fire that does not burn up, no figure of God); Moses before Pharaoh; the people leaving Egypt; Pharaoh's chariots far behind; Moses raising his staff at the sea; the sea splitting into two walls; the people walking through on dry ground; the water closing behind them; the people celebrating on the other side.
4. `david` — David and Goliath: David the shepherd with his sheep; the two armies on the hills; Goliath shouting (close-up); the soldiers hiding behind shields; David with the king and the too-big armour; David picking five stones from a stream; David running forward, sling spinning; the stone flying (speed lines); Goliath falling backwards like a tree with dizzy stars; the soldiers cheering; David smiling at sunset.
5. `joseph` — Joseph: Joseph in his colourful coat; his jealous brothers; Joseph in a dry well looking up; sold to traders with camels; Joseph in Egypt working hard; Joseph explaining Pharaoh's dream (fat and thin cows); Joseph as a ruler in Egyptian clothes; the brothers arrive hungry and bow; Joseph recognises them; the big hug; the father Jacob arriving; the whole family together.
