This audit covers **15 real channels: six direct sleep-learning references, two adjacent channels with explicit sleep episodes or tests, and seven adjacent psychology/philosophy publishers**. It does not present fifteen interchangeable direct sleep competitors. Several channels show recent tracker growth, but the available windows do not justify a fastest-growing league table. Shorts-to-long conversion is largely unverified; producing both formats is not evidence of conversion. Brain After Dark should treat these findings as packaging and format hypotheses within its warm, calm, factual American-English style, rather than permission to copy scripts, claims, art or clips.

**Observation date: October 14, 2026.** October 15 in the title is the requested report date, not a claim that tomorrow's counts were observed. Live-search evidence includes indexed YouTube text, owned destinations, transcript mirrors and third-party trackers; many pages have earlier snapshot dates. Subscriber figures are recorded only where a source displays them, and the table distinguishes a snapshot date from the date of this audit. **High** means seen directly in indexed first-party text or an owned source; **medium** means third-party metadata, mirror or partly corroborated material; **low** means an explicitly labeled inference from limited samples. No source provides private YouTube Analytics, controlled A/B tests, retention or verified conversion.

**Common verification limits apply to every profile:** thumbnail appearance is **not verified**; no thumbnail was genuinely viewed, and creator descriptions of production or suggested thumbnail text do not substitute for visual evidence. Hidden upload tags and pinned comments are **not verified**. First-30-second spoken hooks are **not verified** unless a profile explicitly describes accessible text evidence; even then, exact timing remains unverified. A visible hashtag is labeled a hashtag rather than a hidden tag. Missing playlist, Shorts or description findings mean **not verified**, not absent. These are audit limits, not claims about what a channel actually publishes.
