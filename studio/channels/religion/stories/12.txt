TITLE: Jesus in the Wilderness: The Forty Days of Temptation
THUMB: Even Scripture Tempts
DESC: Three Gospel accounts lead into the wilderness, where hunger, scripture and the meaning of faithfulness meet. This calm Bible documentary explores their distinctive details, the Jewish scriptures behind Jesus’ replies, and the ways early Christians remembered the forty days. We distinguish the Gospel narratives, later interpretation and what historical evidence can establish.
