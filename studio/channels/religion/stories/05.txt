TITLE: Abraham: The Father of Nations
THUMB: The Father of Nations
DESC: Jews call him Avraham, Christians Abraham, Muslims Ibrahim. Belief Through Time follows the story of Genesis 11 to 25, from Ur and Harran to Canaan and Egypt, from the covenant between the pieces to Hagar in the wilderness, the visitors at Mamre, the birth of Isaac and the binding on Mount Moriah, and the cave of Machpelah in Hebron. Then we trace how Abraham was remembered: in later Jewish tradition such as Jubilees and the midrash of the idol shop, in Paul's letters, James and Hebrews, and as Ibrahim in the Quran, with the Kaaba, Hajar and Eid al-Adha. Finally we look at what historians say, from Albright to Thomas L. Thompson and John Van Seters, the camel debate and the search for Ur. We keep scripture, later tradition, historical scholarship and open questions apart.
