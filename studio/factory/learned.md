# Learned rules (shared by every agent)

Written only by the analyst task (cf_analyze) from real performance data and accepted agent proposals. Each rule: date, rule, evidence. Every agent receives these through the context pack, so a lesson learned once applies everywhere at the same time.

## Rules
- 2026-10-13: Too little data yet (channel is days old). Until a video has 7 days of views, follow STYLE.md and trends rules; do not over-fit to single-digit view counts.
- 2026-10-14 (early signal, small numbers, treat as direction not proof): the two best Shorts on Crazy Facts, "Why dread is worse than pain" (264 views, 48 s, 19:00 slot) and "How one word rewrites a memory" (242 views, 43 s, 13:00 slot), plus "Your eye's blind spot" (88), share this pattern: (1) ONE self-contained study told as a mini story, not a topic overview; (2) a counter-intuitive result in the first lines (people chose MORE pain just to avoid waiting; ONE verb changed what people remembered); (3) hard concrete numbers (32 people, 27 of 32, 9 chose a stronger shock sooner; smashed 40.8 mph vs hit 34, 32% vs 14% saw broken glass); (4) the hook text on screen states the surprising claim itself ("27 of 32 chose the shock sooner", "One word changed what they remembered", "You have a hole in your vision"); (5) one clear takeaway at the end that the viewer can use or repeat. Weakest Shorts (4 to 12 views): cut from a long video's intro ("Get comfortable...", sh08_01), or a general topic without a twist, or a very well-known experiment (invisible gorilla). Rule: choose Shorts from the middle of a block where ONE study + ONE twist + numbers sit together; never cut intros or "here is where we are going" agenda parts; open the Short on the surprising result, not on set-up; aim for 40 to 50 s; keep the on-screen hook a claim with a number or a "you" statement. Evidence: studio/yt/status.json 14 Oct 19:54Z, scripts lf08_b06, lf07_b03, lf07_b01 vs lf08_b01, lf08_b04, lf07_b02. Re-check with 7-day data before locking in.
- 2026-10-15: Every Short must be built on ONE named study with at least one concrete number in the first two sentences; do not publish general-topic overview Shorts, and no more than 1 Short per day on the same subtopic (waiting/lines) (evidence: Shorts with a study+number got 253, 269, 106 views; 4 waiting/line overview Shorts got 0-4 views, n=4 vs n=3, direction only)
