@char A12 autumn_overlook | What we mean | By personality
So before we test anything, it's worth asking what we actually mean by personality. In everyday speech, we use the word loosely. She has a big personality. He has no personality at all. But psychologists use it more carefully. Roughly, they mean the patterns in how a person tends to think, feel and behave, patterns that stay fairly steady over time, and that differ from one person to the next.
