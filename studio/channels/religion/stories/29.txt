DESC: How was Adam made from the earth? We hear how Ibn Kathir, Maududi and others read these as stages of one shaping, the debate over 'My two hands' (38:75), the breath of a created spirit (15:29, 32:9), the hadith of a handful from the whole earth, the hollow figure Iblis circled, the first sneeze and greeting, Iblis's claim that fire beats clay (7:12) and Ibn Kathir's reply, the likeness of Jesus and Adam (3:59, 5:110), the stages of every child (23:12-14, 22:5), growing from the earth like a plant (71:17-18, 20:55), and the debated hadith of the days of creation.
