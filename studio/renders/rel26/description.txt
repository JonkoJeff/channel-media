How does the Quran describe mountains, seas and the shaping of the earth? Belief Through Time follows the verses on mountains as pegs and anchors (78:6-7, 16:15, 31:10), the mountains that pass like clouds on the last day, the earth spread out like a carpet, water sent down in measure, the sea made to serve with its ships, fish and pearls, the two seas and the barrier between them (25:53, 55:19-20), the darkness of the deep sea (24:40), the sea splitting like mountains for Moses, the ark on al-Judi, the mountain that trembled at Sinai, and the wall of Dhul-Qarnayn. We read how al-Tabari, Ibn Kathir and al-Qurtubi explained these passages, how Muslim scholars such as Ibn Sina and al-Biruni read the rocks, what plate tectonics, isostasy and seafloor spreading add from modern earth science, and how Muslim thinkers debate the reading of science into scripture.
