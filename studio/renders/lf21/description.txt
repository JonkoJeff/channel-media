Can people really change who they are? A calm, sourced long listen on the psychology of self-transformation: why the idea that character sets like plaster at thirty is wrong, how personality keeps shifting across adult life, the end of history illusion, whether you can choose to change a trait, what learning does to the adult brain, how long habits really take, the willpower myth and the replication that challenged it, identity and possible selves, fresh starts, kindness after a slip, the people who shape us, and the long view of a whole life. The second half is a slow guided wind-down for sleep. Every claim comes from named studies, and the doubts are left in.
