Paul of Tarsus persecuted the first followers of Jesus, and then became the most influential voice of early Christianity. Belief Through Time follows the earliest sources, Paul's own letters and the Book of Acts, to ask what they report about Stephen, the road to Damascus, the years in Arabia, the journeys, the argument over the Gentiles and his last days in Rome, how historians read the accounts, and how Jewish and Muslim traditions see him.
