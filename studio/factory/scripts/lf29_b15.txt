Do you remember the swimmers and golfers from earlier, with their short instructional cues? Elbow up. Slow and steady. Their cues helped them focus on one small thing at a time. Tonight, we'll use the same kind of cues, but instead of helping the body perform, they'll help it rest.

We'll move slowly through the body, from the top of the head down to the feet. For each place, there'll be a short cue, just two or three words. You can repeat it silently, in your own inner voice, or simply let mine do the talking. There's nothing to get right.

Here's how it works. Notice one part of the body. Hear a short cue. Let that place soften as much as it wants to, and no more. Then move on. If you find yourself drifting before we reach your feet, that's not a problem at all. That's the whole idea.
