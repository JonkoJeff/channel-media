@chapter A12 cafe_sunset | A walk by the canal
Imagine you're walking slowly along a quiet canal at dusk. The path is smooth and soft beneath your feet. The water beside you is calm and dark, reflecting the last pale light of the sky. On the other side, old houses lean gently together, their windows glowing warm and yellow. The air is cool, but you're wrapped in a soft coat, and you feel perfectly comfortable.

The canal is very still tonight. Here and there, the light from a window lies across the water in a long golden stripe, trembling just a little. A small arched bridge crosses ahead of you. There's no one else on the path. Just you, and the water, and the soft sound of your own footsteps, unhurried and steady.

@char A10 rooftop_dusk | No hurry tonight | Every step is slow
There's nowhere you need to get to. No meeting, no train to catch, no list waiting at home. Each step is slow and easy. You can stop whenever you like, to look at the water, or at a window, or at the first star appearing above the rooftops. The evening has all the time in the world, and so do you.

@char A15 street_day | A small bridge | The water below
You reach the little bridge and stand in the middle, resting your hands on the cool stone. Below, the water moves so slowly that you can barely see it moving at all. A leaf drifts past, turning gently. You watch it float under the bridge and out the other side, carried along without any effort, wherever the water wants to take it.

@quote A19 rooftop_dusk | Let the day drift past, like a leaf on the water. | A quiet thought
Maybe you can let the day go like that leaf. All the things you did, and all the things you didn't do. Let them float past, gently, and out of sight. You don't need to hold on to them tonight. The water will carry them away, slowly and kindly, and the canal will stay calm and dark and quiet behind them.

@char A13 cafe_sunset | Warm windows | Other quiet evenings
As you walk on, you pass the warm windows of the old houses. Through one, you glimpse a bookshelf and a reading lamp. Through another, a cat curled up on a windowsill, fast asleep. Through a third, nothing at all, just soft curtains and a faint glow. Everyone, it seems, is settling down for the night, just like you.

And your own soft footsteps, steady and calm. Each one is a small, quiet thing. Together, they make a very peaceful evening.

@char A18 attic_rain | A bench by the water | Time to sit
Up ahead, there's a wooden bench by the water. You sit down slowly, and lean back. The wood is smooth, worn soft by many years of people resting just like this. From here, you can see the whole canal stretching away, the lights reflected in the water, the sky growing deeper and deeper blue above the rooftops.

@char A10 bedroom_night | The sky darkens | One star, then more
As you sit, the sky grows darker. One star appears. Then another. Then a few more, scattered softly above the houses. The lights in the windows begin to go out, one by one, as people turn in for the night. And the canal grows even quieter, until the only sound is the gentlest lapping of water against stone.

@quote A03 cabin_fireplace | Slow and calm, like the water. | Your breath
Notice your breath again. Slow and calm, like the water in the canal. In, and out. In, and out. There's no need to count. No need to try. Just let your breath move as quietly as the water does, barely rippling, carrying you a little further into rest with each easy breath.

@char A19 bedroom_night | Heading home | A warm bed waiting
After a while, you stand and walk slowly home along the path. The houses are dark now. The stars are bright. And when you reach your own door, the warmth inside wraps around you. A soft bed is waiting, with a heavy blanket and a cool pillow. You lie down, and close your eyes, and let the quiet of the canal stay with you.
