TITLE: Adam and Eve: The Beginning of Humanity
DESC: A garden, a boundary, and two people learning what it means to be human: Genesis tells the story of Adam and Eve with remarkable economy. Religion Unfolded follows its language and imagery, explores Jewish and Christian readings, and considers what historical scholarship can illuminate—and what remains uncertain.
