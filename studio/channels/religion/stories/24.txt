TITLE: The Heavens as Smoke
DESC: In Surah Fussilat, the heaven is described as smoke before the account turns to its ordering. Religion Unfolded explores this brief passage through its language and the readings of classical Quran commentators. We follow their questions about water, speech and obedience, while keeping scripture, interpretation and historical evidence distinct.
