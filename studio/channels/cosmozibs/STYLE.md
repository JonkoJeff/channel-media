# Cosmozibs style (source of truth; learned rules go to studio/factory/learned_cosmozibs.md)
- Audience: curious kids 9-15 and their parents. English. Warm, upbeat, welcoming like a fun teacher. Never scary, never mean.
- Voices: young boys' voices, slightly raised pitch, no effects. Bolt is a robot only in the picture.
- Every story: hook in 3 s ("This is crazy...", "How could this happen?", "Wait... WHAT?", "If you stood on...") → Zib reacts → setup with a comparison kids know → twist → one big number on screen → Rocky's punchline (= end of the Short) → bonus fact → quiz → transition.
- One fact per story; every number has a source (NASA, ESA, JPL first). Compare big numbers to school buses, football fields, ovens, your house.
- Change the picture every 6-8 seconds.
- Week themes: Mon Weird Worlds · Tue What If...? · Wed Space Mysteries · Thu Time Machine with Tik · Fri Biggest/Fastest/Weirdest · Sat Astronaut Life · Sun Space Myths Busted.
- Never the same hook formula twice in one episode; never the same planet as story 1 two days in a row.
- No accidents with victims, no deaths, no scary aliens. Mysteries end with a calm, curious note.
