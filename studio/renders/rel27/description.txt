What did the angels say when God announced a successor on the earth? Belief Through Time follows the Quranic scene of the angels' question (2:30), the answer 'I know what you do not know', the teaching of the names, the breath and the clay, the command to prostrate, and the refusal of Iblis (7:11-18, 15:26-44, 17:61-65, 18:50, 38:71-85). We read how al-Tabari, Ibn Kathir, al-Razi, al-Zamakhshari and al-Qurtubi explained the angels' question, the meaning of khalifa, and the debate over whether Iblis was an angel or a jinn; the angels who record, guard and pray for people; Gabriel, Michael and the angel of death; the angels in the stories of Abraham, Mary and Badr; related Jewish and Christian traditions such as Genesis Rabbah and Psalm 8; and how thinkers such as Ibn Arabi, al-Ghazali, Rumi and Muhammad Iqbal read the scene.
