You're exhausted, the room is dark, and yet sleep won't come. Why? In this calm, sourced deep dive we follow one imagined woman, Nora, through a sleepless night and test the things we all believe about sleep against the research. We look at the two systems that govern sleep (sleep pressure and the body clock), how caffeine blocks the sleepiness signal, the famous cave experiments, how light and screens talk to the clock, larks and owls, whether you really need eight hours, why a tired body is not the same as a sleepy brain, why trying harder backfires, why a bad night often feels worse than it was, what alcohol really does to the second half of the night, the pre-sleep worry loop and a to-do list study, normal night waking and the history of 'first sleep' and 'second sleep', what actually works for insomnia (CBT-I), the edge of sleep and hypnagogic images, warm feet and cool rooms, sleep trackers, catching up on weekends, and what sleep does for memory and insight. We finish with a gentle toolkit and a slow wind-down. This video is for learning and is not medical advice; if sleep problems last for weeks or affect your days, please talk to a doctor.
