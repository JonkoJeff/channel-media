## 12. Al-Tabari and the Verse

Al-Tabari preserved several interpretations of Quran 21:30 in his commentary. He preferred the explanation relating the opening of the heavens and earth to rain and vegetation, because the verse immediately refers to water and living things. This is a historical exegetical judgment, not a modern scientific account.
