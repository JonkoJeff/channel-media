## Now working
Psychology (long, 5–10 min):
- Thumbnail: white or paper background, 2–3 huge words on the LEFT, one word on a red block, one illustrated figure on the RIGHT, with dark shadow figures showing the emotion. Example: "Psychology of Discipline (How to Make Your Brain Crave Hard Things)", Mind Rove, 4.7x, thumbnail "DO IT / ANYWAY". Also "What Years of Being Alone Does to a Man", Inner Gravity, 1.2x (9.9k views), thumbnail "NEVER / COMING BACK".
- Thumbnail text is never the title. It is the emotional twist ("Do It Anyway", "Never Coming Back", "People Who SKIP Birthdays").
- Title formulas that win: "Why Some People [odd habit]", "What [X] Does to [you/a man]", "Psychology of [X] (How to …)", often with the search suffix "(Psychology Explained)".
- The winners are shorter than ours: 5–8 min (299 s, 369 s, 482 s). Ours run ~10 min.
- Description: the first line repeats the title or asks a "Why do you…?" question. The best performers have chapters.
- Avoid: 25–45 min "Robert Greene / Joker speech" videos (real-person voice or likeness, not our lane).

Psychology (Shorts):
- The biggest outliers are very short (6–15 s): a text list of 3–7 facts, a 🧠 emoji in the title, "7 Psychology Facts That Will Change How You See People" (Mind Decode, 29x, 102k views, 13 s). "3 Name Letters That…" (36x, 39 s) shows that a curiosity number plus "you" works.

Money / AI (long):
- Thumbnail: a face or character plus PROOF (a phone payment screen, a revenue dashboard, a $ amount), with a green check or arrow. "Earn By Training AI / NO INTERVIEW / Up To $5,400/Month" (Om Pathak, 6.7x). "Start with ₹0" plus a dashboard (Aaisha Lately, 4.5x, 138k).
- Titles: "$0 / FREE / No Interview / (Real Results) / Copy Me / laziest way…". Low barrier plus proof.
- Animated finance with a mascot works: FinHamster (3 subs) has a nervous hamster in a tie, a red crash arrow on white, "THE NEXT CRASH ISN'T 2008". This is close to Money Glitch's suit character: emotion plus one symbol (red arrow, fire, cash).
- Never show earnings we can't prove. Proof screens must be real public pages (screen() scenes), never mocked-up income.
