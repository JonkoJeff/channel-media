@study A13 rooftop_dusk | Robert Stickgold and colleagues | Published in Science, 2000 | Had people play the game Tetris for hours, then asked what they saw as they fell asleep
In 2000, Robert Stickgold and colleagues asked volunteers to play the falling-blocks game Tetris for about seven hours, spread over three days. As the volunteers drifted off to sleep, many of them reported seeing the falling shapes, drifting through the dark. Some even felt the shapes turning, as if they were still playing.

@char A03 living_room_day | Even without a memory | A gentle surprise
Then came the surprise. The team also asked people with severe amnesia, who could not remember playing the game at all, to play it. And some of them also reported seeing falling shapes as they fell asleep, though they couldn't say where the pictures had come from. The sleeping brain was replaying something the waking mind could not recall.
